The Science and Policy of Species Conservation

How science, policy, and people shape decisions about threatened and endangered species.

About the Course

From scientific evidence to conservation decisions

This course explores the intersection of science, policy, and conservation by examining how threatened and endangered species are protected in the United States.

We will examine how scientific evidence informs conservation decisions at global, federal, and state levels, with particular emphasis on IUCN listing criteria, the U.S. Endangered Species Act, and the California Endangered Species Act.

Through case studies, discussions, guest speakers, and an applied species-listing project, students will examine both how conservation decisions are made and how scientists can participate effectively in policy processes.
