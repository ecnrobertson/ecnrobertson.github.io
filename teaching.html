Teaching & Course Design

I develop course materials focused on conservation science, policy, and the use of scientific evidence in environmental decision-making. These materials include interactive case studies, data-driven activities, and visual resources designed for undergraduate teaching.

Interactive Case Study

Gray Wolves and the Endangered Species Act

An interactive exploration of gray wolf decline, federal protection, recovery, reintroduction, and the continuing debate over delisting under the Endangered Species Act.

Students examine the history of wolf conservation before taking on stakeholder perspectives and evaluating competing scientific, legal, and management arguments.
