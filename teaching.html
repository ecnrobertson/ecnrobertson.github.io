Teaching & Course Design

I develop courses and teaching materials focused on conservation science, policy, and the use of scientific evidence in environmental decision-making. These materials include interactive case studies, data-driven activities, discussions, and visual resources designed to connect ecological science with real-world conservation problems.

Graduate Course · Fall 2026

ECOL 592: The Science and Policy of Species Conservation

A graduate seminar examining how scientific evidence informs decisions about threatened and endangered species at global, federal, and state levels.

The course combines conservation policy, guest speakers, primary-source case studies, and an applied species-listing project. Course readings, resources, activities, and the semester schedule are available through the course homepage.
